# Chess changelog

## [1.2.1] - 2026-10-29

- Runs in Inner Breeze 2.1.61's smaller app format.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-18

- Paste one PGN game or a FEN position into the study board.
- Import the PGN main line and player details, including custom starting positions and Black-to-move numbering. Comments and side variations are skipped.
- Keep the existing study when an import fails or is canceled, and retain imported studies while switching tabs.
- Review, vary, analyze and export imported positions without changing the saved computer game.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-18

- Ask for a suggested move, take back a turn, flip the board and read its coordinates.
- Play an Expert computer level, with a deeper search and no added evaluation noise.
- Review every move in algebraic notation and try variations in a separate study board.
- Analyze positions with an evaluation bar, completed search depth and a suggested move.
- Copy a game's PGN or the study position's FEN for use in other chess tools.
- New game labels travel with Chess in all twelve languages, preserving existing saves and friend games.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-10-18

- Play the same Chess game from Arcade or Inner Breeze, keeping existing
  saved-game formats and friend games in Inner Breeze.
- Game updates carry their own translated text and preview picture.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-17

- Chess is out of beta: More apps lists it for everyone, without Show beta apps.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

- Play chess against the computer at three levels, as White or Black, with
  the board's full rules: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate,
  stalemate and the draws by repetition, fifty moves and insufficient
  material. The game is saved and continues after a restart.
- Play a friend from your friend list: invite them, and they accept or
  decline. Moves reach the other player through your Inner Breeze account.
- Chess is not installed by default; add it from More apps.
