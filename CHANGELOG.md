# Arcade changelog

## 0.6.0

- Pushy is now Shovey, with Classic, Island and a level Editor.
- Choose joystick or swipe controls; the joystick shows on touch screens.
- Level 121 is new, upside down, with a fresh route.

## 0.5.2

- No lines between Pushy's tiles in Inner Breeze, and new layouts for levels 2, 4 and 5.

## 0.5.1

- Pushy Classic in pixel art like the original, with Pushy seen from above.
- Soft sound effects in Pushy on desktop.
- Restart always restarts the puzzle you see; no move counters.
- Arcade opens in Inner Breeze again and shows a gamepad icon.

## 0.5.0

- Pushy has new artwork and new puzzle layouts in both Classic and Island,
  and every island has a new name.
- Opening Pushy Classic or Island goes straight to your newest unlocked level.

## 0.4.0

- Choose Pushy Classic or Pushy Island from Pushy, including the standalone desktop app.
- View whole-board previews for unlocked puzzles, with clear lock and completion icons.
- Open games through the shared Apps preview cards and vertical options menu.
- Keep existing Chess and Classic saves while moving between the library and games.

## 0.3.1

- Pushy automatically continues after a win and starts selected puzzles from the beginning.
- Continue recovers saves left at a completed puzzle.
- Pushy shadow puzzles and several board interactions now work correctly.

## 0.3.0

- Import a PGN game or FEN position into Chess study, with player details and the correct starting move number.
- Review and analyze imported positions, then copy them back to other chess tools.
- Keep an imported study while switching between Chess tabs, with clear feedback for invalid input.

## 0.2.1

- Pushy adds Continue and a level selector with completion marks.
- Completing a Pushy puzzle unlocks the next one and keeps progress across restarts.

## 0.2.0

- Chess adds hints, turn takebacks, an Expert opponent, board coordinates and flipping.
- Study a game move by move, try variations, analyze positions and copy PGN or FEN.
- Game updates retain their translated text across compatible Arcade and Inner Breeze hosts.

## 0.1.2

- Open Pushy from the game library with keyboard and on-screen controls,
  restart, and an object guide.
- Continue the same Pushy position after leaving the game or restarting.
- The two shadow puzzles at 165–166 can be viewed but remain paused while
  their rules are being verified.

## 0.1.1

- Choose games from a grid of cards with real game previews.
- Return to the library with the top Back button and continue the same game.
- Chess uses one game package across Arcade and Inner Breeze, with its own
  translations and compatibility checks that preserve existing games.

## 0.1.0

- Open Chess from Arcade and play the computer at three difficulty levels.
- Continue a saved local game after closing and reopening Arcade.
- Chess remains available in Inner Breeze for thoughtful play.
